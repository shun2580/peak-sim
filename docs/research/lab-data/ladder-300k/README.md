Host clock, 2026-11-16 (the app and web logs use UTC, 9 hours behind). Facts only; nothing here is compared with the prediction, and no pass or fail is written here.

- `s-1-300000/` to `s-5-300000/`: the ladder records as written by `measure-ladder` (`ladder.json`, per step `rung-NN-<rps>/` with the record, the k6 output, the settings read, the memory and oom readings before and after, the write counters). Byte-identical to the originals (`diff -r` gave no difference).
  - s-1: 09:01:52 to 10:27:44, exit 0. s-2: 10:27:47 to 11:44:59, exit 0. s-3: 11:45:04 to 13:47:33, exit 0. s-4: 13:53:11 to 15:10:21, exit 0.
  - s-5: 15:17:25 to 17:19:51, exit 19 (`check-oom` stopped it in step 9, rps 0.45). Steps 1 to 8 have records; step 9 has none. `oom-stop-s-5/` holds what was read at the stop (`inspect-app.txt`, `inspect-db.txt`, `mem-sequence.txt`, the tail of the app log).
- `s-1.output.txt` to `s-5.output.txt`: the output of each scenario. `s-1.not-started-0900.output.txt`: the first start of s-1 at 08:59:57, which ended with exit 2 at 09:00:11 before any load because `targets-300000.json` was missing; `targets-300000.output.txt` is the run of `bin/targets` that made it. The directory of that attempt (a dry run and a `tables-before.tsv`) is not copied.
- `chain.log`, `chain.log.before-s-5`: the times of the chain (start, end and exit of each scenario, the two recreations of the app and web containers, the hold before s-4). `B-start.txt`, `B-start-2.txt`: the times the chain was started. `watch.log`, `watch.seen`: the watcher that looked at the output directory (the last lines of `watch.seen` are the step end markers seen).
- `scripts/`: `chain-B.sh`, `stats.sh` (the version in place from 12:05, with a wait before s-4 so that the app and web containers could be recreated between s-3 and s-4; `stats.sh.orig-T6B2` is the version before), `watch_steps.sh`, `watch_done.sh`. See "Changes to the copies" below.
- `limits-before/`, `limits-after-open/`, `limits-after-b-s5/`: the php-fpm side values (`check-limits`) before B, after the limits were opened, and after s-5 stopped (before the settings were put back). `compose-B.diff`: the diff of the two compose files that made B.
- `recreate/`: the readings around the two recreations of the app and web containers (before s-4 at 13:52:34 and before s-5 at 15:16:22): `use-db` output, `check-limits` after, the `oom` readings before, and the sha256 of the compose diff before each (unchanged).

- `stats-scenarios.tsv`, `stats-steps.tsv`, `dbstats/`: `mysql.innodb_table_stats` (last_update, n_rows) read before and after each scenario and after each step, and the two readings taken just before B. No ANALYZE was run by the measurement.
- `orig/sha256.txt`: the sha256 of the two compose files before B (`zz-lab.ini`, `lab.conf`).

## App and web logs (`logs/`)

The app container's log is very large (the PHP error dumps of the failing requests carry long argument lists): about 1.6 GB for the five scenarios, so the full files are not kept here. `logs/*.dated-lines.txt` keep, byte for byte and in order, only the lines that begin with a date stamp (php-fpm NOTICE and WARNING lines, `PHP Warning` and `PHP Fatal error` lines, and the first line of each Laravel error); the lines that continue a dump (stack frames and argument lists) are left out. `logs/MANIFEST.tsv` lists, for each original log, its bytes, its lines and its sha256, and the bytes and lines kept. `logs/app-after-s-N.at` holds the time each copy was taken (UTC). The web logs (`web-after-s-N.txt`) are kept whole.
